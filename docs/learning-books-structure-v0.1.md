**Status:** Runtime hierarchy added to the two learner content packs; item-level lesson coverage remains partial.  
**Scope:** Ali (KG2), Aboudy (Grade 2).  
**Rule:** A publisher's unit title is used only where its scope/contents sequence was verified. Other outlines are original AAS2 companion structures and must never be presented as publisher chapter titles.

## Learner-facing navigation

1. The home screen presents learning areas only.
2. Selecting a learning area opens its book directly when exactly one book is available.
3. If a learning area has multiple books (Aboudy's Mathematics), show the book choices.
4. A book opens its units/chapters, then lessons, then lesson activities.
5. Additional practice remains a separate section on the learning-area screen; it is not mixed into the book's lesson list.
6. If a lesson has no approved runtime activity yet, show a clear preparation message. Do not invent or silently duplicate activities to make a lesson appear populated.

## Learning values and child experience

Apply the saved AAS2 principles to every authored activity:
- Question-first and visual-answer interaction; large touch targets and minimal reading load for KG2.
- No punishment, no score deduction for an error, no streak pressure, and no competition between Ali and Aboudy.
- Feedback remains encouraging and instructional; rewards reflect learning progress/mastery, not taps or screen time.
- The child receives one retry after the first incorrect answer. If the second attempt is also incorrect, the activity offers the next-question action.
- Correctness is determined by reviewed answer keys and deterministic rules, never by an unverified AI judgement.
- Core learning works offline; children's learning data stays on-device unless a separate privacy review and explicit approval authorise otherwise.
- Arabic support is not forced into the child's English learning questions; app chrome may be Arabic/English.
