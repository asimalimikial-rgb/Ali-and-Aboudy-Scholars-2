# Textbook Digital Access Investigation v0.2

**Checked:** 23 November 2026  
**Project:** Ali & Aboudy Scholars 2 (AAS2-NEW)  
**Repository branch:** `foundation/slice-0-android`  
**Scope:** Find the actual books, full digital editions, publisher previews, licensed readers, and legitimate purchase routes for the KG2 and Grade 2 Macmillan titles.

## Executive result

This investigation found three publisher-origin Macmillan digital-book records on BlinkLearning:

1. **Macmillan Science 2 ebook** — full book structure and 18-topic contents are listed; the reader explicitly says a licence is required and directs the user to the school or publisher.
2. **Macmillan Mathematics 2A ebook** — 12-unit contents are listed; BlinkLearning offers a demo request.
3. **Macmillan Mathematics 2B ebook** — 12-unit contents are listed; BlinkLearning offers a demo request. Macmillan's current course page also exposes a sample download/view entry for 2B.

These are real digital-book records, not cover-only listings. However, the public pages do **not** expose the complete readable/downloadable books without authorization. The public table of contents is not a full-book copy.

No publisher-authorized, freely downloadable complete PDF was verified for any of the seven titles below. The practical full-book routes are a valid code/account, school or library licence, or purchase from a legitimate seller. No login, paywall, licence, or DRM restriction was bypassed.

## 3. How to obtain the complete digital books lawfully

### Macmillan Education Everywhere (MEE)
- Platform: https://www.macmillaneducationeverywhere.com/
- Help centre: https://www.macmillaneducationeverywhere.com/help-centre
- Macmillan's help page says the access code is normally printed inside the book/booklet. Student subscriptions generally last 15 months and teacher subscriptions 24 months after activation; exact duration depends on the course.
- If a family owns the printed eBook pack, check the inside cover for the code. Do not share the code in chat. Activate it privately in the official MEE account and verify that the exact course appears in My Bookshelf.
- If no code is included, request an authorized individual/family purchase or school/library licence from Macmillan or the seller.

### Macmillan India / Xpress
- For Hop Skip and Jump and Maths Xpress, ask Macmillan Education India whether the exact ISBN/kit has an eBook licence, whether it is parent/student-accessible, and whether an authorized demo can be issued.
- Publisher contact published on its site: `info@macmillaneducation.com`, `+91 120 4000100`.
- The Xpress digital portal also advertises demo requests; the portal's successful operation and the specific Senior KG book's inclusion were not confirmed in this review.

## 4. Sources deliberately excluded

- An Internet Archive record titled “Macmillan mathematics” (2009) was inspected. It is an 18-volume set with different ISBNs (for example, `9780230732872`) and marked access-restricted/print-disabled. It is not the identified 2023 Level 2A/2B eBook pack, so it is not treated as a copy of the requested book.
- Scribd, user-uploaded scans, file-sharing mirrors and unidentified PDFs were not treated as lawful book sources.
- A listing, search snippet, cover image, contents page or sample does not establish permission to download, reproduce or redistribute a complete book.
