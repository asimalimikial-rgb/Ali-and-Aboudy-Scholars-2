## Purpose and handling

This register brings together the identified KG2 and Grade 2 Macmillan books, their cover references, ISBNs, official publisher pages, and lawful digital sample/access routes.

**Important rights boundary:** Macmillan textbooks and their covers are copyrighted. The repository stores links and metadata only; it does not mirror, redistribute, or embed complete copyrighted books. A public sample or a catalogue PDF is not a licence to redistribute its contents. Full eBooks should be accessed through a valid publisher purchase/code/account or a library/school licence.

The cover images below are externally hosted reference previews. They are not copied into this repository, and their exact print/ISBN match is marked separately. If a publisher-hosted cover image is not publicly exposed, a retailer image is only a provisional visual identifier.

The 2025 catalogue lists Literacy Skill Sheets, Numeracy Skill Sheets, General Awareness Skill Sheets, My Second Book of Phonics, My Third Book of Stories, My Third Book of Rhymes and Songs, My Second Book of Handwriting, My Third Book of Art and Craft, and My Third Book of SEL/STEM/Creative Coding. ### MEE access
Macmillan Education Everywhere is the current official digital route. The publisher's help centre says access codes are normally inside the book/booklet or supplied by the institution/local representative. After activation, a resource appears in My Bookshelf. The Download control enables offline access when offered for that resource; not every resource is guaranteed to have a downloadable PDF. The legacy LMS page states sunset was scheduled for 31 July 2026.
