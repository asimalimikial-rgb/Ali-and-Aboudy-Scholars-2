## 1. Purpose and design constraints

This document begins the curriculum-design phase for Ali & Aboudy Scholars 2. It defines the initial subject architecture, source hierarchy, skill domains, and authoring rules. It does not reproduce textbook content and does not claim that the current pilot packs constitute a complete syllabus.

The product has one shared learning engine and two distinct pathways:

- **Ali — KG2 / Senior KG:** English & Early Literacy; Mathematics.
- **Aboudy — Grade 2:** English; Mathematics; Science as an AAS2 enrichment pathway.

Core constraints:

- Offline-first learning; no network dependency for lessons, assessment, or progress.
- English is the language of learning content. Arabic is used for concise instructions or optional support where pedagogically useful; device language must not silently turn an English-learning lesson into an Arabic lesson.
- Original AAS2 explanations, examples, questions, options, feedback, stories, diagrams, and illustrations.
- Sudanese everyday contexts where appropriate, without presenting a foreign curriculum as the Sudanese national curriculum.
- Each child has a separate progression, activity history, mastery evidence, and recommendations.
- Do not expand the question bank until the Skill Map and curriculum crosswalk are reviewed.

## 3. Initial Skill Map — Ali (KG2)

### English & Early Literacy

| Skill domain | Proposed progression | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Listening and oral language | Listen to short instructions, identify familiar objects/actions, answer simple questions | Follows an age-appropriate instruction and responds meaningfully |
| Vocabulary | Build everyday vocabulary through people, home, school, play, food, animals, and surroundings | Names, recognises, and uses target words in context |
| Phonological awareness | Rhyme, syllables, sound noticing, initial-sound identification, then blending/segmenting when ready | Identifies a target sound or sound pattern without relying only on picture guessing |
| Phonics and decoding | Learn letter forms and sound correspondences in a verified systematic sequence; blend simple CVC words after prerequisite sounds are secure | Matches graphemes to phonemes and decodes unfamiliar, age-appropriate examples |
| Early reading comprehension | Picture-supported stories, sequencing, characters, setting, simple recall and prediction | Retells or answers questions about a short, unfamiliar story |
| Handwriting and early writing | Fine-motor control, letter formation, name writing, copying familiar words, then simple captions | Forms recognisable letters and writes a short meaningful label/caption |

**Important sequencing gate:** do not invent a Jolly Phonics or other letter-introduction order. Confirm the exact Hop Skip and Jump phonics book or an explicitly selected phonics programme before assigning letter-by-letter sequence and audio.

### Mathematics

| Skill domain | Proposed progression | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Counting and cardinality | One-to-one counting, stable order, cardinality, counting on from a given number | Counts a set accurately and understands the last number names the quantity |
| Number recognition | Recognise, name, represent, and order numerals within the confirmed Senior KG scope | Matches numeral, spoken number, and quantity |
| Comparing quantities | More/fewer/equal; compare and order small sets | Compares sets and explains using quantity language |
| Composition and early operations | Compose/decompose quantities; concrete joining and separating with objects/pictures | Models and solves a simple addition/subtraction situation |
| Patterns and classification | Sort by one or more attributes; copy, continue, and create repeating patterns | Identifies the rule and extends a pattern |
| Shape and spatial reasoning | Recognise common 2D shapes; describe visible attributes and position | Identifies shapes by attributes, not colour alone |
| Measurement foundations | Compare length, mass, capacity, and time informally using direct comparison and familiar units | Selects a sensible comparison method and uses comparative language |

Exact numeral ranges and the order of units remain pending inspection of the specific Senior KG books. Do not hard-code a presumed range such as 1–20 until confirmed.

## 4. Initial Skill Map — Aboudy (Grade 2)

### English

Use SMILE Starter 2 to preserve local topic/context alignment and English World Level 2 as a broader international progression reference. Do not merge their unit sequences mechanically.

**Local topic anchors in SMILE Starter 2:** Home; My Family; Toys and Games; Animals; Food and Drink.

**English World Level 2 scope indicators:** action verbs; playground equipment; sports and hobbies; clothes; time phrases; body parts; food/drink/containers; animals; sea creatures; beach/island/boat contexts. Its phonics sequence includes magic-e patterns, initial consonant blends, and long-vowel patterns including ee, oo, ai, and ea.

| Skill domain | Proposed progression | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Phonics and decoding | Consolidate short-vowel/CVC foundations; progress through verified magic-e, consonant blends, and long-vowel patterns | Decodes unfamiliar words with taught patterns in and out of context |
| Reading fluency | Accurate phrase-level reading with appropriate pacing and expression | Reads a short, level-appropriate passage accurately and meaningfully |
| Vocabulary | Topic vocabulary plus action words, time, places, body, food, animals, and descriptive language | Selects and uses words appropriately in novel contexts |
| Grammar and sentence structure | Questions/answers, present simple and present continuous, pronouns/possessives, there is/are, prepositions, basic past forms | Produces and recognises meaningful, grammatically appropriate sentences |
| Reading comprehension | Stories, descriptions, poems, information with labels, emails, diaries, and routines | Identifies explicit information, sequences events, and makes simple supported inferences |
| Writing | Sentence construction, descriptions, routines, short connected writing, capitalisation and punctuation | Writes a short coherent text for a clear purpose |

The exact grammar/phonics sequence should follow the confirmed book edition and a prerequisite map, not merely the order of isolated scope-and-sequence rows.

### Mathematics

The official Sudan Grade 2 Mathematics revised edition 2021 identifies these six broad units:

1. Numbers to 1,000.
2. Addition and subtraction.
3. Multiplication.
4. Division.
5. Measurement.
6. Geometry.

The book introduction describes the course as extending number and basic operations, including multiplication tables, measurement, and geometry. Its copyright notice prohibits copying or adapting its content without written permission; use it for topic-level alignment only.

| Skill domain | Proposed progression | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Place value and number sense | Read, represent, compose/decompose, compare, and order numbers to 1,000 | Explains the value of a digit by its hundreds/tens/ones position |
| Addition | Add within the verified range, first with place-value models then written strategies | Solves and checks an addition problem, including regrouping when taught |
| Subtraction | Subtract within the verified range using concrete/pictorial then written strategies | Solves a subtraction problem and explains the regrouping strategy |
| Multiplication | Equal groups, repeated addition, arrays, and multiplication facts/tables in a gradual sequence | Represents multiplication in more than one way |
| Division | Equal sharing and grouping; connect division to multiplication where appropriate | Models a division situation and interprets the quotient |
| Measurement | Compare and measure using the units and instruments confirmed in the exact source edition | Chooses and uses an appropriate measurement unit |
| Geometry | Identify, describe, compare, and classify common shapes and spatial relationships | Uses attributes and mathematical language to describe shapes |

### Science — Core AAS2 subject (Macmillan Science Level 2)

Macmillan Science Level 2 is a **core subject in the AAS2 Grade 2 pathway**, with its own digital book, units, lessons, Skill Map, question bank, assessment, progress tracking, and review schedule. This is an AAS2 product decision, not a claim that Science is listed as a compulsory subject in every Sudanese Grade 2 school. The public Sudan Learning Passport Grade 2 listing reviewed on 18 November shows Arabic, Arts, English, Islamic Studies, and Mathematics; it does not list Science. That listing is a boundary on what we claim about the national catalogue, not a reason to demote Macmillan Science inside AAS2.

| Skill domain | Proposed progression | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Observation and classification | Notice similarities/differences; sort living things, materials, and observable features | Groups examples using a stated observable property |
| Living things and habitats | Identify basic needs and suitable habitats; connect organisms to environments | Matches an organism to a plausible habitat and gives a simple reason |
| Plants and growth | Observe plant parts and basic growth needs | Describes a change over time from observations |
| Materials and properties | Compare observable properties such as hard/soft, rough/smooth, flexible/rigid | Selects a material for a simple purpose using its properties |
| Inquiry and evidence | Ask a question, predict, observe, record, and communicate a result | Gives a simple claim supported by an observation |

NOAA/Woods Hole Sea Grant's Grade 2 Plant and Animal Habitats resource is recorded as Public Domain in its institutional repository. Use its learning topic as a source for independently written AAS2 activities; review any embedded or separately sourced assets individually.

## 5. Vertical progression between the two pathways

| Domain | KG2 readiness | Grade 2 progression |
|---|---|---|
| English sound/word reading | Sound awareness, letter-sound correspondence, early blending and CVC decoding | More complex vowel patterns, blends, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension |
| English writing | Letter formation, name, words, short captions | Complete sentences, descriptions, routines, and short connected texts |
| Number | Quantity, counting, numeral recognition, comparison | Place value to 1,000, ordering, and multi-digit operations |
| Operations | Concrete joining/separating | Addition/subtraction strategies, multiplication, and division |
| Geometry/measurement | Shape recognition, informal comparison | Attribute-based descriptions, measurement units, and problem solving |
| Science inquiry | Observe, notice, classify, describe | Ask, predict, compare evidence, and explain a simple relationship |

This is a developmental progression map, not an assumption that every child enters KG2 or Grade 2 with identical readiness. A short diagnostic should identify prerequisites before lessons are assigned.

## 6. Standard lesson design

Each lesson should follow a consistent but age-adjusted learning cycle:

1. **Retrieval:** briefly revisit a prerequisite skill.
2. **Learning intention:** one clear, observable outcome.
3. **Explicit teaching:** a small explanation with a concrete or visual model.
4. **Guided practice:** supported examples with feedback.
5. **Independent practice:** a fresh item that tests the same skill.
6. **Transfer:** apply the skill in a different but familiar context.
7. **Review:** schedule a later retrieval opportunity and record mastery evidence.

KG2 lessons should be short, visual, tactile in concept, and low in text density. Grade 2 lessons can use multi-step tasks and richer reading, but should still make the learning goal explicit.

## 7. Mastery and assessment rules — draft

- A single correct answer is not sufficient evidence of durable mastery.
- Record skill-level attempts, answer choice, correctness, hint use, feedback snapshot, content-pack version, and timestamp.
- Distinguish independent success from hint-assisted success.
- Use more than one item form and more than one session before promoting a skill to secure mastery.
- Include delayed retrieval to check retention.
- Diagnose the misconception behind an error and change representation or scaffold; do not simply repeat the same question.
- Numeric mastery thresholds remain provisional until the Skill Map and assessment evidence model are reviewed.

## 8. Current pilot-pack gap analysis

The current KG2 pilot pack contains 4 skills and 8 bilingual activity records: counting to five, common shapes, plant needs, and initial sounds. It is a technical pilot, not the complete English/Math curriculum. In particular, it needs a systematic phonics/early reading pathway and broader numeracy progression.

The current Grade 2 pilot pack contains 6 skills and 12 bilingual activity records: three mathematics skills, one habitat-science skill, and two English skills. It is also a technical pilot, not the complete English/Math/Science curriculum.

The current content model stores a single activity language and selects the activity matching the device language. This needs a product-level review because the approved direction is English learning content with concise Arabic instructions/support, rather than automatically replacing the English lesson with a fully Arabic version when the device is set to Arabic. Resolve the bilingual content model before large-scale content authoring.

- The first edition (2019–2021) of IM K–12 Math is recorded as CC BY 4.0; preserve attribution, link the licence, and state modifications if adapting covered material. Do not confuse it with later non-commercial editions.
- QKLG material may be adapted only where it is QCAA-created and covered by CC BY 4.0, subject to its exceptions.
- NOAA Public Domain status applies to the identified repository item; separately sourced images/audio remain subject to separate review.
- Each production skill must map to source-reference IDs and carry a review status.
