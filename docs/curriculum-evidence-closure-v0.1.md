## 1. Evidence grading

- **Verified publisher identity:** publisher/catalogue identifies a product or ISBN.
- **Verified scope:** an official Scope and Sequence or equivalent publisher document was inspected.
- **Catalogue-only:** product components or broad pedagogy are listed, but detailed learner outcomes are not established.
- **Family copy pending:** no cover/ISBN/photo or physical copy from the family has been matched.
- **Official local curriculum pending:** no current, grade-specific official document was verified in this review.
- **REFERENCE_ONLY:** may support topic alignment; does not authorize reproduction of protected expression or assets.

## 3. Sudan curriculum evidence review

### 3.1 Grade 2

**Official public-platform snapshot:** Sudan Learning Passport currently displays Grade 2 subjects as Arabic, Arts, English, Islamic Studies and Mathematics. Science is not listed under Grade 2 in that platform snapshot. Science appears in the displayed Grade 3 and later lists.

Source: https://sudan.learningpassport.org/

**Interpretation boundary:** the Learning Passport is a published platform listing, not a complete legal statement of every Sudanese school's current timetable or the entire national syllabus. The absence of Science from that Grade 2 listing must not be rewritten as proof that no school teaches Science in Grade 2.

**Ministry portal review:** the Ministry of Education portal currently exposes a 2026–2027 school calendar and selected primary/middle curriculum entries, but the menu inspected did not provide a current Grade 2 syllabus or KG2 framework. This is a documentation gap, not proof that no such official document exists.

Source: https://gmoe.gov.sd/

**Historical/local documents:** Sudan Grade 2 Mathematics revised edition 2021 and SMILE Starter 2 Grade 2 second edition 2020 are useful secondary alignment references. Their dates and rights status must remain visible; neither is evidence by itself of the current 2026–2027 national curriculum.

- Grade 2 Mathematics revised edition 2021: https://www.mdl.edu.sd/img/bookpdf/math2_1659464927.pdf
- SMILE Starter 2 Grade 2, second edition 2020: https://www.mdl.edu.sd/img/bookpdf/english2_1664176727.pdf
- British Council SMILE project context: https://africa.teachingenglish.org.uk/classroom/curriculum-specific-resources/sudan/smile-series

### 3.2 KG2 / pre-primary

- The Ministry portal includes pre-primary education administration and recent 2026 reporting about kindergarten administration and implementation activity.
- No current official KG2 learning framework, scope-and-sequence, or subject outcome document was verified in this review.
- Macmillan India Senior KG catalogue records are publisher/product evidence only; they are not evidence of Sudan's national KG2 curriculum.

**Status:** official Sudan KG2 curriculum evidence remains open.

### 3.3 Science product-scope decision

- Aboudy Grade 2 Science remains a **Core AAS2 subject** because this is an explicit product decision.
- The product must not claim that Science is compulsory in every Sudanese Grade 2 school.
- Macmillan Science Level 2 is the selected primary Science reference; local curriculum alignment remains a separate evidence stream.
- The first Science MVP slice should be drawn from verified scope topics and original AAS2 activities, with safety review. The complete 18-topic scope does not have to ship in the first release.

## 5. Rights and provenance

- All Macmillan and Sudanese textbook references remain REFERENCE_ONLY.
- ISBN verification does not grant a reproduction or adaptation licence.
- Catalogue descriptions and Scope and Sequence topics may be used to align independently authored learning outcomes; do not copy the protected expression, examples, questions, artwork, layouts, audio scripts or teacher materials.
- Every original AAS2 item must carry a canonical sourceReferenceId, exact Skill ID, review status, and per-asset provenance.
- A source's licence does not automatically cover third-party images, fonts, audio or embedded assets.

## 7. Completion decision

The available evidence is sufficient to maintain a provisional Macmillan-first crosswalk and to author original topic-level drafts for English World Level 2 and Macmillan Science Level 2. It is **not sufficient** to claim exact family-book alignment for the five paths, to finalize Ali's skill sequence, or to finalize Aboudy's Mathematics 2A sequence.

No missing source is silently filled from a neighbouring level, old teacher pack, unofficial upload or international benchmark. The outstanding family-copy and official Sudan documents remain explicit blockers for claims of exact local alignment, but do not invalidate the evidence-graded draft.
