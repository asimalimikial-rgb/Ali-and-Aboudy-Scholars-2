## Scope

This original AAS2 unit follows the scope-level sequence for Macmillan Science Level 2 topics 10–14. The textbook remains a reference-only source; this unit does not reproduce textbook text, questions, illustrations, diagrams or page layouts.

## Design controls

- 40 original English single-choice items, each with three unique options, one answer key, and a rationale for every option.
- Mix of diagnostic, guided, independent, transfer and retrieval assessment phases.
- Difficulty values 1–2 are provisional, not calibrated.
- Safety controls prohibit looking directly at the Sun, touching unknown insects or substances, tasting unknown foods/plants, approaching smoke or heat, and requiring any child to taste food.
- The current app renderer supports single-choice only; these 40 drafts therefore use that supported interaction type. This does not imply that all science outcomes are fully assessed.
