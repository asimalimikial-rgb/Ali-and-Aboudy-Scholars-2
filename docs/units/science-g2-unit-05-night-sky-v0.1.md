Original items introduce the Moon as Earth's natural satellite, reflected sunlight, stars as distant light sources, impact craters and constellations as apparent patterns.

**Safety:** Night-sky observation is adult-supervised from a safe ground-level place. Never look directly at the Sun, including through optical devices. An adult prepares any crater model; model materials must not be tasted.

The unit illustration is staged as an original SVG. Audio generation, visual QA, astronomy accuracy, age suitability, accessibility and answer-key review remain open.
