# AAS2 Curriculum Crosswalk v0.1 — Macmillan-first

**Status:** Evidence-graded working crosswalk; not final page-by-page curriculum approval.  
**Scope:** Ali KG2 and Aboudy Grade 2.  
**Curriculum hierarchy:** Macmillan is the primary curriculum backbone. Sudanese SMILE and Ministry/NCCER materials are secondary local-alignment references. Cambridge and other international frameworks are benchmarks only.  
**Rights rule:** Macmillan, SMILE, and Sudanese textbook materials remain `REFERENCE_ONLY` unless a separate item-specific licence or written permission is verified. All AAS2 explanations, examples, questions, feedback, diagrams, and media must be original.

## 1. How to read this crosswalk

The crosswalk distinguishes curriculum coverage from evidence strength. A topic appearing in a catalogue or unit title is not proof that a learner outcome is taught to depth or mastered.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Covered** | Direct, sufficiently specific evidence supports the stated learning outcome in the reviewed source. This does not mean the child has mastered it. |
| **Partially covered** | The source supports only part of the skill, a limited set of examples, or a component-level match. |
| **Enrichment** | A useful AAS2 extension beyond the verified core scope, clearly labelled as such. |
| **Not evidenced / pending** | The reviewed source does not yet provide enough evidence. This is not the same as Missing. |
| **Potential gap** | A benchmark or product requirement suggests an area to investigate; do not call it Missing until the full relevant source and edition have been checked. |

**Evidence boundary:** publisher catalogues, official Scope and Sequence documents, and available indexes have different evidential weight. The actual books used by the family have not been matched by cover/ISBN in this review. No page-level pacing or complete textbook coverage is claimed.

## 4. Cross-curriculum and local-alignment rules

1. Macmillan is the primary curriculum backbone for the selected AAS2 books and learning paths.
2. SMILE is a secondary local-alignment source for selected Grade 2 English themes; it does not control the sequence or replace Macmillan.
3. Sudanese Ministry/NCCER textbooks may inform topic-level local alignment only where the rights register classifies them as `REFERENCE_ONLY`.
4. Cambridge EY3, Primary English 0057/0058, Mathematics 0096 and Science 0097 are benchmarks, not evidence of the learner's school curriculum.
5. Do not equate a publisher level with a school grade or learner readiness automatically.
6. Keep family-edition verification separate from publisher-catalogue verification.
7. Use **Not evidenced / pending** rather than **Missing** whenever the complete relevant scope or exact edition has not been reviewed.
8. A topic being present in a Scope and Sequence does not prove learner mastery; assessment evidence is separate.
