# AAS2 Vertical Skill Map v0.1 — KG2 to Grade 2

**Status:** Working, evidence-graded progression map. It is not a claim that the two children are at identical developmental stages or that one book level automatically maps to the next.  
**Review date:** 23 November 2026  
**Canonical detailed registry:** docs/skill-registry-v0.1.md  
**Curriculum source register:** docs/content-source-register.md

## 1. Mapping principles

1. The map describes a plausible developmental progression from Ali's KG2 pathway toward the domains Aboudy studies in Grade 2. It is not a shared grade sequence and does not place either child by age alone.
2. Each learner keeps a separate sequence, diagnostic baseline and mastery evidence.
3. A prerequisite is a learning dependency only when the skill registry supports it; it must not be invented from topic similarity.
4. Macmillan is the primary nominated book backbone. Catalogue-only evidence cannot establish a precise skill sequence.
5. Each learning session is question-first; diagnostic question precedes any micro-teaching.
6. Mastery is provisional until the child independently demonstrates the skill on varied items across sessions and later retrieval.
7. Exact numeric mastery thresholds and review intervals remain uncalibrated.

## 4. Diagnostic evidence, mastery and retention policy

For each skill, retain the following evidence where relevant:

- skillId, activityId, content version and learner path.
- Assessment phase: DIAGNOSTIC, GUIDED, INDEPENDENT, TRANSFER or REVIEW.
- Response and correctness.
- Hint/support level and whether the response was independent.
- Misconception code tied to the distractor/response pattern.
- Evidence type: selected response, oral response, practical observation, short writing or interaction trace.
- Timestamp and later retrieval outcome.

Mastery labels remain provisional:

- **Not assessed:** no usable evidence.
- **Emerging:** response succeeds with modelling or substantial support.
- **Developing:** independent evidence exists but is inconsistent.
- **Secure (provisional):** independent success on varied items across sessions plus later retrieval.
- **Review due:** delayed retrieval is absent, unsuccessful or stale.

Do not set a universal percentage or attempt count before item calibration. Do not count hint-assisted success as independent mastery.

## 5. Source confidence and curriculum rules

- CONFIRMED_SCOPE: direct official Scope and Sequence support.
- PARTIAL_SCOPE: only some elements or task types are evidenced.
- CATALOGUE_ONLY: product/component description, not a detailed sequence.
- PENDING_EDITION: exact family edition or matching scope unverified.
- BENCHMARK_ONLY: external framework, not the family's curriculum.
- AAS2_PRODUCT_SCOPE: explicit product decision independent of a national curriculum claim.

Macmillan is the primary nominated reference; SMILE and Sudanese textbooks are secondary local-alignment references; Cambridge/QKLG/ELOF are benchmarks. Keep the Sudan Grade 2 Science question separate from the AAS2 product decision: Learning Passport's public Grade 2 listing does not show Science, but that snapshot does not prove absence in all schools.
